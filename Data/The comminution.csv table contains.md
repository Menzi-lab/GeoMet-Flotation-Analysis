The comminution.csv table contains measurements associated with the DWT and BWI indexes. The flotation.csv table contains measurements related to the metallurgical recovery of copper in a locked cycled test LCT and the drillholes.csv table contains chemical analysis data along with the geospatial coordinates of the samples.



The analytical Plan

1\. Flotation analysis: Explore fr, xr, copper (Cu ppm), iron (Fe ppm), sulphur (S ppm) and other available measurements. We'll establish what fr and xr represent before interpreting them.

2.Comminution analysis: Investigate F80, P80, M, A and the th variables after checking their definitions. Explore whether ore chemistry is associated with the measured comminution properties.

3.Drill-hole analysis: Investigate the distributions of copper, iron, sulphur and other elements, and examine their variation across drill-hole coordinates and identifiers.





Step 1 — Define the project

The goal is to investigate the flotation dataset, understand its quality, explore relationships between elemental composition and flotation results, and identify insights that could support metallurgical decision-making.

Potential questions to investigate:

**- What are the distributions of the measured elements?**

**-** **How do copper (Cu ppm), iron (Fe ppm) and sulphur (S ppm) vary across the samples?**

**- Are there relationships between elemental concentrations and the flotation variables fr and xr?**

**- Which samples have unusual values or distinctive chemical compositions?**

**- What insights can be supported by the available data?**
